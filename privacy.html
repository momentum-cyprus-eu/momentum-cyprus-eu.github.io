Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Momentum Capital Group ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect through this website, why we collect it, and the rights you have under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the UK GDPR.

1. Who we are

Momentum Capital Group helps buyers from the UK and Europe purchase property in Cyprus. We are the controller of the personal data described in this policy. You can reach us at capitalgroupmomentum@gmail.com or on +972 54-770-9499.

2. Data you give us

  • Contact form: your name, phone or WhatsApp number and country, and, if you choose to add them, your email address, what you are looking for, your budget and your message. The form does not send anything by itself: it opens WhatsApp with your details filled in, and nothing reaches us until you press send there.
  • WhatsApp chat: the answers you tap (your goal, budget, timing and where you are buying from) and, if you add it, your first name. They are turned into a message that opens in WhatsApp. Nothing is sent until you press send there.
  • Email or phone: anything you choose to tell us when you contact us directly.

3. Data collected automatically

  • No cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. This website does not use them.
  • Hosting. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data, such as your IP address and browser type, for security.
  • Fonts and code libraries. Our fonts load from Google Fonts, and some of our code loads from the jsDelivr and unpkg networks. These providers receive your IP address when the page loads.
  • Your device. Your browser remembers, for the current visit only, that you have already seen the opening animation. This stays on your device and is not sent to us.

4. Why we use your data, and our legal basis

  • To answer your enquiry, arrange a consultation and send you information about properties you asked about. Legal basis: steps you ask us to take before a possible contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • To keep a record of our conversations and protect our legal position. Legal basis: our legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)).
  • To send you news or offers, only if you agree. Legal basis: your consent (Article 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time.

We do not sell or rent your personal data, and we do not use it for automated decision-making or profiling.

5. Who we share it with

  • With your knowledge, the professionals involved in a purchase you ask us to pursue, such as independent lawyers, developers and sellers in Cyprus.
  • The services we use to talk with you, such as email (Google) and WhatsApp (Meta).
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

6. International transfers

We work from Israel. The European Commission and the UK have both recognised Israel as providing an adequate level of protection for personal data. Our service providers, such as Google, Meta and GitHub, may process data in other countries, including the United States, under their own safeguards, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry data for up to 24 months after our last contact with you, unless you become a client or the law requires us to keep it longer. After that, we delete it.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, correct it, have it erased, restrict or object to its use, receive it in a portable format, and withdraw your consent at any time. To use any of these rights, write to capitalgroupmomentum@gmail.com. We will reply within one month.

You can also complain to the data protection authority in your country. In the UK, that is the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). In Cyprus, it is the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect your data. No email or internet transmission is completely secure, so please do not send us passports or other sensitive documents by email unless we have agreed on a safe way to do so.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
