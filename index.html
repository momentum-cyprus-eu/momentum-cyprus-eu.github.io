It all began with family property deals that brought me to Paphos. The deeper I got into the market, the clearer it became how fast the city was changing and which areas held real potential. So I moved there to learn the market from the inside.

For a year I lived Paphos day to day: its neighbourhoods, purchase and rental prices, where demand was growing and which new projects were worth a buyer’s attention. Along the way I built close relationships with the island’s leading developers and companies.

Buying in Cyprus after Brexit? It’s simpler than you think.

Cyprus has long been a favourite with British buyers, and it still is. UK citizens can buy property here with a straightforward permission process, which your lawyer handles as part of the purchase. Qualifying purchases can also open a route to residency.

A familiar feel

A legal system based on English law, English spoken everywhere, and they even drive on the left.

A large British community

Especially in Paphos, with clubs, English-speaking doctors and schools.

Short flights home

Direct flights to London and other UK cities.

An EU home in the Mediterranean sun.

As an EU citizen, you can buy property in Cyprus with the same rights as locals, live here freely and pay in euros. Whether you’re coming from Germany, the Netherlands, Scandinavia or anywhere else in Europe, we’ll make the process clear and simple.
