Buying in Cyprus after Brexit? It’s simpler than you think.

Cyprus has long been a favourite with British buyers, and it still is. UK citizens can buy property here with a straightforward permission process, which your lawyer handles as part of the purchase. Qualifying purchases can also open a route to residency.

  • A familiar feel A legal system based on English law, English spoken everywhere, and they even drive on the left.
  • A large British community Especially in Paphos, with clubs, English-speaking doctors and schools.
  • Short flights home Direct flights to London and other UK cities.
  • Residency support We’ll guide you to the right route for your situation.

An EU home in the Mediterranean sun.

As an EU citizen, you can buy property in Cyprus with the same rights as locals, live here freely and pay in euros. Whether you’re coming from Germany, the Netherlands, Scandinavia or anywhere else in Europe, we’ll make the process clear and simple.

  • No extra permissions EU citizens buy on the same terms as Cypriots.
  • The euro No currency risk and no exchange fees.
  • Freedom to live here Move, work or retire without a residency investment.
  • Support in your language English and German, with documents explained clearly.
